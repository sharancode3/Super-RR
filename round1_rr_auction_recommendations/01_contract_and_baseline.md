# Stage 01 — Read the contract and reproduce the baseline

## Goal

Freeze the question, inputs, evaluation targets and experiment plan. Use START.md, 00_audit_and_sources.md, the supplied case PDF, the official Unstop listing and the saved final-review run as inputs.

## Execute

1. Write a compact requirements matrix. Record two ordered tens, highest final rating first, no repeat within a discipline, exact agreement between form and report, an explained pressure definition, usual versus pressure performance, fairness, consistency, and an RR role and auction case. Track form spelling separately from cricket role. Classify the competition requirements, project choices and unresolved market facts. The 24-ball floor, raw rate sign, and domestic preference are project choices.
2. Compare the seven supplied hashes with the workspace and baseline manifests. The Downloads copy is an independently matching reference. Preserve original input files, any archived outputs, and the old shortlist scenarios. Verify raw, market and saved artifact hashes separately. Inventory the generated registry CSV in the workspace raw folder and confirm the CLI does not consume it as official input. Save exact executable, seed, dependency versions, source or commit digest and artifact settings in the new run.
3. Trace the normal CLI from ledger and role inference to pressure labels, player impacts, fit, compare, robustness, rank and export. Identify which outputs the old full run generated, which evidence was added separately, and which outputs the current comparison actually uses. Reproduce the saved raw ranks, form order and relevant validation metrics as an archived baseline. Record its runtime. A matching dependency fingerprint can justify reusing an exact artifact.
4. Define the two forecast questions. The primary scouting target is future IPL player-opportunity impact under a declared high-pressure role deployment, in adjusted runs per 12 legal balls. The companion target is usual impact over comparable non-pressure opportunities with the same feasible phase and matchup mix. Specify the reference player and phase/innings distribution, the minimum opportunity grain, handling of sparse and short stints, and a shared validation target. Specify how the unit differs from total runs produced over a fixed intended workload. Define the independent match-state evaluation target if a win probability model is used to derive pressure.
5. Define one final RR policy family. Require a verified player identity, supported discipline and a credible auction action at the cutoff. Apply the same acquisition evidence standard to every player. Use observed RR players as role benchmarks. Set how conditional routes and unknown statuses will be handled. Preserve a statistical rank and all candidate evidence in internal diagnostics. Commit to one final ranked list per discipline.
6. Freeze the candidate comparison design before inspecting new outcomes. Use earlier IPL rolling origins for training and tuning, 2025 as reused validation, and 2026 as the already-inspected retrospective. The existing 0.05 Q25 loss allowance and +1.0 player-weighted shortlist hurdle are saved controls. Declare an acceptable calibration range, practical improvement margin, assessable-coverage criterion, and comparison basis before running new alternatives. Include tests for the old hard IPL floor and positive rate rule and one proposed uncertainty-aware rule. Document how conflict between quantile error, shortlist quality and exposure will be resolved. Keep the stronger simpler model when evidence is tied.

## Loop

Reproduce the archived inputs, current metrics, and both role orders. For a discrepancy identify whether it comes from a version, target, role cutoff, selector, market input, or export. Fix the setup or log the exact reason the historical artifact cannot be reproduced byte-for-byte. Recheck the requirement matrix against the supplied brief and public page. Record the evaluation protocol before moving on.

## Exit and redirect

The baseline and raw inputs are independently verified or precisely reconciled. The forecast and ranking targets, as-of dates, test protocol, eligibility treatment and maximum number of trials are written down. Go to [Stage 02](02_data_pressure_and_impact.md).
