# Stage 05 — Compare models and calibrate confidence

## Replay the actual decision

1. Run whole-match chronological folds. Freeze the target, exact cutoff, candidate pool, pre-cutoff roles, and prior match-state references. Reproduce the earlier IPL origins, 2025 reused validation, and 2026 retrospective. Keep the 2023 H2 T20I window as a transfer check. Verify historical role and market facts using dates. Tests should append or alter future results, roles, team changes or transactions and assert unchanged earlier fit features, eligibility and scores.
2. Compare model forecasts on identical held-out rows and a fixed reference outcome. If a pressure transformation is a separate experiment, report label differences and evaluate competing models on the common reference. Score mean error and bias, Q25 pinball loss and empirical coverage against 25%, and optional interval width, coverage and interval score for a declared 80% interval. The primary ex-ante forecast uses a pre-cutoff phase/role mix. Evaluation using a realized mix is labeled as conditional. Show ordinary and pressure results, and positive short and longer workloads as slices.
3. Replay both shortlists under the exact same selector and feasibility inputs for each model and policy. Include Challenger D under the positive-IPL control, which the saved positive-screen batting comparison omitted. Report later IPL impact using player and ball weights, with assessable counts out of ten and precise reasons for zero or thin outcomes. Compare like-for-like eligible and assessable subsets where possible. Present conditional impact and opportunity coverage together. Compare paired differences on common match draws, with confidence intervals and counts. Where historical markets use a roster proxy, label that limitation.

## Quantities to report for every model and player prediction

4. Emit a model-level calibration table by discipline, fold, and supported role/workload group with match count, player-opportunity count, pinball loss, lower-quarter coverage, interval coverage and width, MAE, bias and paired uncertainty. For small groups show the support and the pooled calibration used. Describe the calibration date range and which predictions share it.
5. Emit a player forecast row for every finalist and serious alternative from each compared model. Required fields are model ID, player ID, discipline, role, origin, forecast unit, pressure mean and Q25, usual mean, their difference, an empirically checked prediction interval, and observed IPL/T20I/emerging exposures. Include a confidence statement sourced to actual out-of-time calibration and how much of the player's forecast uses pooled or transferred evidence. Track whether the interval concerns one future opportunity or latent mean ability.
6. If reporting a probability, define its event before training. An example is the chance that next-opportunity adjusted pressure impact meets an earlier IPL role benchmark. Obtain it from a calibrated predictive distribution or a calibrated event model. Evaluate Brier score, calibration bins, and event and sample counts on out-of-time data. Use the same benchmark definition across models. A player's probability of beating an RR incumbent needs a justified joint comparison under matching deployment. Mark probabilities unavailable where evidence cannot calibrate them.
7. Measure selection stability independently. Resample whole matches in shared draws, preserving competition and season as appropriate. Refit or recompute the relevant model statistics and execute the same final selector in each resample. Record number of draws, selected draws, eligible draws, unconditional and eligible-conditional selection frequencies, and rank percentiles among ranked draws. Record exclusions separately and use null rank for an unranked player. Replace raw-rank-based HIGH_CONFIDENCE labels and the 999 sentinel in the user-facing confidence tables. Check Monte Carlo stability with repeated seeds or a larger run before publishing a close number.
8. Stress-test P75/P80/P85 and continuous pressure, IPL-only and transfer, recent versus all history, role mix and matchup support, proxy target errors, standout-match removal, and plausible squad changes. Give the exact slate changes and reason. Keep the model choice fixed during sensitivity checks unless the test is explicitly a model comparison.

## Loop

When a forecast fails calibration, inspect the target, the source of uncertainty and the model's short or transfer strata. Recalibrate using earlier out-of-fold data, then replay on the same held-out rows. When a comparison changes candidate pool or assessed exposure, report both effects and rerun the paired common comparison. Follow the frozen promotion rules. Record a tie or failure with measured uncertainty.

Every compared model has an honest calibration and performance record. Every potential pick has uncertainty and stability under the declared policy, or a specific documented evidence gap.
