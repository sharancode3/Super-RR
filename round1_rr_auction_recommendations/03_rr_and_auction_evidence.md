# Stage 03 — Build the RR and auction evidence

## Match actual players to roles

1. Establish a dated 2026 RR baseline using official squad and transaction sources and the pack's 2026 IPL match records. Confirm franchise and player IDs. For every job capture recent opportunity volume, phase and innings mix, usual and pressure impact, availability uncertainty, and likely slot cost. Example jobs are opening aggression, keeper cover, middle-over spin batting, Indian late finishing, new-ball swing, attacking middle-over spin and death pace. Measure role need against the current group and likely playing options. An apparent gap over one match or one short stint requires a wider check.
2. Build equivalent role depth for every franchise whose players could enter the shortlist. Use 2025–26 IPL, all relevant IPL years, and appropriate transfer data to show the player's normal deployment. Include incumbents and reserves, change of club, overseas status, replacement contract when documented, and recent workload or recovery when verified. Compare possible targets against a relevant RR player and role-matched rivals on the same phases and dates. A rival's depth and investment can support a conditional release scenario. Keep its assumption visible. A well-known name and raw rating alone do not establish an auction action.
3. Use comparable cricket tasks when making cases. Reproduce Bethell's powerplay and middle-over splits before calling him a No. 4–5 spin option. Compare Aniket and other Indian finishers with the late-over RR group. Use Vaibhav and Jaiswal as real opening benchmarks. Compare Malinga, Bhuvneshwar, Siraj, Hazlewood and other eligible options within intended new-ball or death jobs. Check spin options against the RR middle-overs group. Use these named cases as regression evidence, with the same requirements for the rest of the pool.

## Source and model availability correctly

4. Implement a market evidence view as of a precise timestamp. For each fact record canonical player ID, action and franchise, effective and publication dates, source URL and what it verifies. Keep current contract, release, trade, registration, final register inclusion, retirement, clearance and fitness distinct. Prefer IPL and franchise announcements and national governing bodies. Label reliable reporting and uncertainty as such. Correct known citation/date mismatches using the sources in the audit. Never promote a registry note to a league rule without supporting evidence.
5. Use the same performance criteria for RR incumbents and rivals. An eligible incumbent can be backed in the squad if retained. A rival under contract can be considered for an auction bid if the club releases the player and the player enters the auction. Confirmed trades update club and depth with their actual dates. Record internal untouchable exclusions as entrant decisions. Keep proven ineligibility, credible conditional availability and missing evidence distinguishable.
6. Verify Indian or overseas designation using official player or auction evidence for finalists and near-cutoff alternatives. Unknown stays unknown. Check starting XI rules, squad slots and any purse in their correct season and scope. Use current official evidence where available and label roster or purse assumptions as scenarios. Apply the entrant's preference for domestic depth to comparable RR options under a declared rule, with an explicit opportunity cost. A 10+10 candidate list represents alternative recommendations and is evaluated as a shortlist. A lineup illustration must satisfy the relevant playing and squad conditions.
7. Use historical pre-auction release and squad snapshots to replay feasibility where the data exists. Apply facts known at each origin. The pack's prior-season roster is a roster proxy. It does not establish past release status. Record missing historical scope and the set of comparable candidates. Use explicit conditional cases for future availability. Estimate a numerical release probability only if a properly labeled, temporally evaluated historical panel supports its calibration.

## Loop

Cross-check every material claim against the cited fact and date. Fix contradictions at the source registry and rerun the market view. Where only a conditional path is supported, keep its trigger and evidence. If a player case uses the wrong phase or job, recompute the relevant role comparison before revising the recommendation.
