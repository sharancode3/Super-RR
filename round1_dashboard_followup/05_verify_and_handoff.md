## Agent prompt

Use the existing build, audit and browser verification scripts where they measure the right behavior. Run two independent acceptance passes on the version actually served on the agreed local port. Review numerical integrity and the evaluator's experience after a clean production build.

### Pass 1 · Data, training and provenance

- Verify original pack and ledger hashes. Run the derived export twice and confirm stable outputs. Verify the approved recommendation and raw-score population. Compare at least five high-value cases including both of Jadeja's role rows, the two leaders and one near-cutoff pick. Locate genuine exceptions and log their reason. Sort rank tables by their documented score and deterministic tie-breakers. Audit counts, cohort filters, label names and dated auction states.
- Confirm no RR-only rule affected the Model rankings population or ordering. Verify overlapping model scores are the same in views created from the same snapshot. Trace examples of additions, exclusions and rank changes to the real rule. Match the displayed RR list exactly to the selected snapshot. A new model must carry the paired backtest and decision record from Stage 02; a retained baseline carries the conclusion and limits of the new challenge.
- Cross-check each worked match fact with the ledger and official match source. Confirm the site shows 167 and Match 54 in the revised example, the correct pressure formula and comparison periods, real recipe names and actual sample sizes. Verify names and photos. Check no public payload includes raw deliveries, proprietary inputs or personal paths. Confirm all rendered percent labels are tied to the specified cohort and snapshot.
- Run the applicable type, lint, production build and model or exporter tests. Test the scripts from normal entry points, confirm both artifact manifests are reproducible, and check served asset filenames against the built version. Treat fixture-level flags and a documented baseline shortage of assessed auction players as numerical evidence to show. Compare the site against the approval fixture again after any format or export change.

### Pass 2 · Evaluator path and visual checks

- Check production views at approximately 320px, 390px, 768px, 1024px and 1440px. Capture the main entry, one complete rank path per role, the model-to-RR transition and the Methodology chapter. Exercise sticky navigation, table scrolling where appropriate, evidence panels and deep links. The reasoning remains readable when viewport zoom increases to 200%.
- Review keyboard-only use, focus, reduced-motion, slow images and smooth scroll. Confirm app-wide emoji removal including generated surface text. Verify portraits do not jump, clip or intrude into text. Check Jadeja uses one identity and one portrait family throughout the two role cards. Verify the correct credit appears.
- Test that someone can answer within a short judging pass: who leads each list, what we measured against ordinary performance, which ranking is raw, how an RR constraint can change a pick, and what a 42.8% selection frequency means. If the reader needs model jargon to get those answers, shorten the visible sentence and move the technical text into detail.
- Verify serving from the correct build rather than relying on a source diff. Take screenshots and record the actual served URL and browser versions, numerical checks and performance observations. Stop after the defined checks pass. When a check fails, return to [Stage 00](00_reconcile_current_build.md) for provenance, [Stage 01](01_model_rankings_to_rr.md) for selection logic, [Stage 02](02_bowling_backtest_and_confidence.md) for model evidence, [Stage 03](03_methodology_and_evidence.md) for claims and [Stage 04](04_ui_portraits_and_motion.md) for interaction and imagery. Repair and rerun the relevant check and both acceptance passes after material changes.

### Handoff

**Pass** when both acceptance passes succeed. Return local build/run instructions, screenshot locations, the snapshot date, whether the bowling baseline kept its place, any actual selection differences, a compact comparison of the two ranking populations, and exactly what remains uncertain. If one check is blocked by a genuine dependency, deliver verified portions and the specific step still required. Keep the handoff aligned with the actual build.
