## What the evaluator can actually use

Round 1 asks for two ordered top tens and a methodology report. The brief calls for performance under pressure against each player's usual level, comparable contexts, consistency across matches, and evidence for the ordering. The form uses role-specific dropdowns and a PDF/PPTX upload. RR asks which players to back at the IPL 2027 auction. The brief gives no budget allocation, published ground-truth ranking, or actual procurement commitment. The entrant owns the final decisions and must keep the proprietary pack private. Money is one real constraint. A recruit also needs an available roster slot, a role that adds something to RR, eligibility for that auction, enough expected playing availability, and credible evidence in the role. The IPL's ₹157 crore figure for 2027 is the announced total salary cap with incremental performance pay and match fees. RR's spendable auction balance depends on its actual roster and commitments. ## Which methods to test next

- Use simple phase/tier and B0/B1 baselines to separate ordinary skill from evidence of a pressure-specific change. Estimate the change in comparable roles, phases and match situations. Sparse match effects should borrow strength, with uncertainty shown separately from observed variation. Davis, Perera and Swartz use hierarchical empirical Bayes in a T20 simulator. Bandyopadhyay's T20 preprint uses contextual runs and state-dependent dismissal values, which support a transparent target. Neither supplies validation for this pack's 2027 forecasts. [T20 simulator](https://www.sfu.ca/~tswartz/papers/t20sim.pdf) · [Context-adjusted T20 player evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18020v2)
- Prioritize a calibrated hierarchical Student-t or robust empirical-Bayes distribution as a challenger to the current B0/B1. Compare with a small quantile tree model on strictly past-only features. A bounded CatBoost trial is justified only if categorical context or interactions add measured value. Use matched time folds and proper scoring, then measure actual shortlist decisions separately. A model for next-match outcome variance and a measure of confidence in player skill answer different questions. [Gneiting and Raftery on proper scoring rules](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437) · [CatBoost quantile objective](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/concepts/loss-functions-regression)
- Use observed-match pooling, verified public context, and valid historical feature transformations to improve coverage. Use whole-match resampling to measure uncertainty. Fabricating extra examples of rare pressure events would change the question and inflate support. A new rule, data source, or model earns adoption by improving fair earlier-fold comparisons. The primary path is forward assessment and verified provenance. Perfect future ranks cannot be established from the supplied historical observations.
