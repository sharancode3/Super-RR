## Make comparisons fair

1. Use forward-chaining by whole fixture and date. Refit all upstream learned inputs on earlier rows at each evaluation origin, including match-state models, pressure references, role priors, transfer weights and calibration parameters. Use 2024 rolling evidence and earlier feasible training blocks for development. Replay 2025 IPL with each decision made from earlier data. Label 2026 IPL as a previously inspected retrospective stress check and keep it out of tuning. Confirm the actual cohort for each fold.
2. Use common eligible assessment rows keyed by canonical player, match, discipline and the declared opportunity unit. Evaluate all positive legal pressure workloads and report the 1–5 and 6-plus slices. Include ordinary predictions at compatible contexts and support. Show differences by phase, innings, league evidence, recent role and player experience where counts permit. Expose changes in the denominator. If a pressure or target repair changes label units or opportunities, refit the controls on the corrected assessment target and use the saved legacy result as context.
3. Measure Q25 pinball loss with `e = y - q25` and `max(0.25*e, -0.75*e)`. Report empirical Q25 coverage against 25%, absolute error, signed bias, empirical Q10–Q90 interval coverage against 80%, and interval width. If fitting a probability of positive role-relative impact, validate reliability and Brier score on the same outcome definition. Show the sample size and match-block uncertainty. State which metrics are match, player or ball weighted.
4. Calibrate on earlier out-of-fold predictions. Compare raw and calibrated forecasts on the same later rows. Start with a pooled residual calibration, then use a workload or role adjustment only when enough earlier matches support it. Marginal Q25 coverage can look acceptable while short opportunities fail, so show both. Check ordered quantiles, consistent coverage with width, and empirical performance through season drift. Describe any distributional assumption and scope of the interval.

## Replay the production decision rule

5. Compare all fitted candidates under the same incumbent-inclusive version of the positive direct IPL screen. Reproduce the historical evidence-only screen for continuity and report the model selection versus production rule discrepancy. Then test one alternative that reduces sensitivity to a near-zero direct rate. Use a role-adjusted empirical Bayes estimate and match-level uncertainty. For example, estimate the probability that same-role IPL impact exceeds a predeclared materially negative floor, conditional on direct workload and transfer evidence. Shrinking a rate toward zero alone preserves its sign, so the rule must actually handle uncertainty or a practical margin. Use prior-era franchise maps and state which market facts the historical replay can approximate.
6. Compare top tens using realized later adjusted impact on the same match window, with both player-weighted and ball-weighted values, the exact assessable counts, and reasons for thin or absent opportunities. A player with no future pressure exposure is missing an evaluation outcome. Show the exposure result alongside realized performance. Use paired match resamples for slate differences and common-row forecast loss. A changed candidate mix can shift assessability. Report the support and bootstrap interval before concluding that a small mean difference is actionable.
7. Use the promotion criteria frozen in Stage 01. Require a credible validation gain on a decision metric with acceptable calibration and exposure, or a material forecast gain with a competitive slate and support. Compare on earlier rolling evidence as well as the predeclared replay. Preserve B0 for bowling and the existing batting control if the new candidate misses the gate. Document the result of every attempted challenger, including a tie or rejected model. Do not promote a candidate because a preferred name moved or a metric improved only after choosing the window.

## Quantify uncertainty of the actual top tens

8. Run a whole-match cluster bootstrap using the same final selector. Preserve sampled match multiplicity and resample weights. Recompute model or rating evidence within each draw and state which upstream labels or roles are frozen. Track eligibility count, probability of appearance in the ten under this resampling, rank distribution conditional on eligibility and pairwise order for near ties. Cover every actual top-ten pick and the material reserves. Store the number of draws, seed and Monte Carlo sensitivity. Handle ineligible draws explicitly and leave rank intervals undefined when no eligible draw exists.
9. Retest 15% and 25% pressure thresholds, IPL-only versus pooled, recent-only support, one-match removal, target-proxy sensitivity where material, and thin-role and workload gates. Compare the same test on prior run and new run. Explain whether a shift comes from eligibility, the estimate, uncertainty, or the RR application rule. Replace threshold-based confidence words with measured quantities, denominators and the specific scenario. Emit diagnostics and confidence for each compared model plus the selected 20.
