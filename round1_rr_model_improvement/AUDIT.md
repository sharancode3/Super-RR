## Modeling decisions supported by the research

- **Use match context before adding complexity.** Thomson, Perera and Swartz show how runs required, wickets and balls shape cricket performance comparisons in chases. Ganjoo's T20 analysis illustrates adjusted run impact and win probability as related measures. Use a pre-ball pressure measure and a player-level, role-adjusted impact target. Compare sensitivity to chase and first-innings contexts using the same outcome cohort. [Contextual batting and bowling](https://www.journals.ac.za/sasj/article/view/4951) · [T20 Metrics](https://hganjoo.github.io/t20basics/)
- **Start with partial pooling and short-stint modeling.** The existing RPP and delivery-hazard work can be reused. A regularized player baseline and pressure term can borrow evidence by role and phase. A two-part runs and dismissal model is worth testing if the simple distribution still misses early dismissals. Stevenson and Brewer motivate changing ability and survival in Test cricket. Transfer to this IPL task must earn support on our held-out IPL opportunities. [Cricket ability and survival](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssc/article/70/2/481/7033927)
- **Match evaluation to the prediction.** Use pinball loss for Q25, coverage and width for prediction intervals, and paired match blocks for comparisons. The pack has observed seasons rather than organizer labels for the winning twenty. Treat already examined 2026 outcomes as retrospective. A chronological residual calibrator can be assessed empirically. Standard conformal coverage depends on exchangeability, and time drift requires an appropriate method and an explicit check.
