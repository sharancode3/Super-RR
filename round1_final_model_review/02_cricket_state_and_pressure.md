# Stage 02 — Repair match state, pressure and impact

## Work on the normal pipeline

Trace rr_pressure/ledger.py, models.py, pressure.py and impact.py. Keep baseline and corrected artifacts side by side in fresh output directories. Use small test fixtures for cricket invariants and use only observed data for fitting and reporting.

## Ledger and event attribution

1. Assert match, innings and delivery keys, match ordering, join cardinality, phase boundaries and monotone pre/post states. Reconcile scorebook totals and legal-ball counts where independent scorecards are available. Test wides, no-balls with off-bat runs, no-ball plus secondary extras, byes/leg-byes, penalties, a carryover free hit, a wide stumping, striker and nonstriker run-outs, and a dismissal on an innings-ending ball. Check one-over to next-over transitions. Quarantine ambiguous states with reasons and a sensitivity view.
2. Verify exactly how the numerator and denominator count events. Distinguish team runs, batter runs, bowler-charged runs, bowler-credited wickets, and striker dismissals. With per-12-legal-ball bowling impact, an illegal delivery can contribute runs or a credited wicket without consuming a legal ball. Ensure expected runs and wickets use an opportunity definition consistent with this accounting. Document treatment of penalties independent of the bowler and innings with zero legal exposure. Verify opponent and role joins using stable IDs.
3. Maintain target provenance. The current ledger has one verified target and 805 reconstructed chases. Audit a small predeclared set of independently sourced exact targets, prioritizing finals, close chases and representative IPL dates as feasible. Record source URL, fixture ID, match date, target, discrepancy and verified date in a separate input. Use a target-known-only sensitivity where sample size supports it and use plausible proxy error scenarios otherwise. Sensitivity scenarios are computation inputs, with no extra training deliveries.

## Pressure and win-value invariants

4. Fix the confirmed chase successor-state issue in pressure.py. Compute the legal and illegal successor outcomes from the pre-ball state. A target reached is a batting-side win. A completed chase below the tie score is a loss. Represent an equal regulation score as a tie with an explicit, documented value for the chosen estimand and sensitivity. Target reached takes precedence when a legal ball exhausts the resource. A first-innings end sets a total and transitions to the chase context. Use a learned probability for a genuinely undecided state and an exact terminal value when the state determines the result. Cover last ball, last wicket, target reached with a wide, free-hit dismissal rules, and tie. Reproduce match 50375 and explain any change to its leverage, label and impact.
5. Verify the transition distribution, normalized probabilities and all observed outcome classes relevant to the estimand. The existing leverage is the expected absolute deviation of successor values from their probability-weighted mean. Begin with the corrected current outcome model. Evaluate whether common combined events, three runs or illegal delivery branches change materially. If an expansion is needed, use a small fitted transition table with partial pooling across innings, phase, competition and free-hit state. Avoid individual-player outcomes or actual successor state when computing pre-ball leverage. Refit the historical P80 thresholds by innings and tier, and use P75/P85 and a simple chase-state pressure definition as sensitivity controls.
6. Verify the peer expectation and wicket-value arithmetic. The batter's impact is actual minus expected batter runs minus the state-dependent wicket cost times excess dismissal risk realized. Bowling uses expected minus actual bowler-charged runs plus the state-dependent wicket value times excess credited wickets realized. Recompute at least one concrete batter and one bowler example from the ledger, with inputs and full precision saved. Check sign, units, missing values and phase/pitch applicability. Document how the same role/phase context supports usual-versus-pressure comparisons.
7. Regenerate the impacted labels and player-match or player-match-phase aggregates. Measure counts and label switches by inning, phase, competition, legal status and proxy source. Show how the six leading contested cases and the shortlist boundary move under the corrected implementation. Preserve reference cutoffs so that a future match or outcome mutation cannot change past features.

## Loop

Start with small exact-outcome tests and then check real matches. On failure return to event attribution or state reconstruction before changing the model. After a pressure or label fix, regenerate peer expectations, impact targets and affected later stages. On weak source coverage keep the calibrated proxy with the source limitation visible and quantify sensitivity. Pass changes based on reproducible cricket semantics and measured effects.
