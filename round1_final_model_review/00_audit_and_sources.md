## Research to apply

- Bandyopadhyay's 2026 T20 preprint uses context-adjusted scoring and run-valued dismissals with partial pooling. Borrow the idea of a comparable runs scale and explicit wicket value. Retain our pack's attribution rules and verify them. This paper is evidence for the measurement architecture, with performance on this task to be established locally. [T20 context-adjusted evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18020v2)
- The 2024 T20I pressure-index study motivates a second-innings sensitivity based on balls, wickets and required scoring. Use it as a reference for testing whether a chase pressure definition is cricket-plausible. A first-innings definition needs its own match-value logic. [T20I chase pressure](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.3233/JSA-240792)
- CAMP motivates contextual player evaluation with opponent and match circumstances. Add opposition or venue only where the pack has reliable pre-cutoff data and the ablation improves performance. [CAMP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13700)
- Stevenson and Brewer model dismissal as a hazard that varies through an innings. Their application is Test cricket. Use the survival concept for a T20 short-stint diagnostic, and evaluate it on the pack. [Batting dismissal hazard](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssc/article/70/2/481/7033927)
- Pathak et al., published 10 November 2026, use LightGBM and an MLP with match context for T20I XI recommendations. Their task, inputs and validation differ from a future IPL pressure shortlist. Use their architecture as support for evaluating context and role fit. Justify any nonlinear challenger with a same-data backtest. [Context-aware T20I selection](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-75023-z)
- Gneiting and Raftery support proper scoring of forecasts. Use pinball loss for the lower quantile, pair it with empirical calibration, and use a proper interval score if reporting prediction intervals. Use rolling-origin evaluation to mimic the information available at selection time. [Proper scoring rules](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437) · [Rolling-origin evaluation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169207000000650)
