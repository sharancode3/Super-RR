# Stage 06 — Convert pressure evidence into an RR decision

## Use a dated candidate universe

1. Start with canonical identities from the supplied data and any available official Unstop player pool. Verify exact dropdown text where the entrant supplies search evidence or where read-only verification is authorized. Record the spelling, field, date and verification state. The ability to find the same name in both dropdowns does not establish its cricket role. Check role from observed deployment and a credible player source for uncertain cases. A genuine all-rounder needs independent role support in each list they enter.
2. Implement an explicit market as-of date in the registry and shortlist inputs. Resolve official facts by publication and effective date, stable ID, provenance and explicit precedence. Fail or flag contradictory authoritative records. Track IPL team, RR incumbent status, retention/release, trade, registration, fitness and clearance in distinct fields. Use official IPL, franchise, and governing-body records first. When using credible reporting, label what the publication confirms and what remains unresolved. Derive disposition from evidence with a reason. A rival's strength or salary can support a release-scenario judgement, with its assumption visible.
3. Verify Indian versus overseas status for selected and near-cutoff players from official auction or squad records. Retain an explicit unknown state. Replace substring/default classification where it affects selection. Use the official starting XI and squad constraints only for the scenario they apply to. Confirm sourced RR slots and purse when available and use declared plausible scenarios where they are pending.

## Apply the entrant's decisions consistently

4. Produce the model performance view and two runnable RR scenarios. The primary review is retain-inclusive, applying the same pressure and evidence standard to every RR incumbent. Vaibhav is the entrant's retain recommendation and is No. 1 in the saved batting performance ranking. Show his recomputed rank and strongest evidence. Riyan and other incumbents receive the same treatment. A confirmed RR release changes the route to the applicable auction scenario. The acquisition-only comparison contains realistic external targets with explicit conditions. Record how any organizer clarification changes the eligible form view.
5. Compare the primary review order with an Indian-majority alternative. Six Indian and four overseas names in a list of ten implements the stated preference. Apply it through a declared role-fit decision rule and show each replacement, model score, uncertainty and role match. Begin with a same-role Indian option near the cutoff. Quantify performance and deployment cost before recommending a change. Keep a baseline unconstrained order so the entrant can see what the preference changes. If credible candidates or evidence are insufficient to reach the preference, state the trade-off and present the best supported alternatives.
6. Match players to actual RR jobs. For batters, compare opening/keeping, middle-overs spin hitting, and late finishing. For bowlers, compare powerplay seam, middle-overs spin, and death pace, including variation and arm angle where verified. Use the same phase and recent date window for the candidate, incumbent and reserve. Separate exposure in the intended phase from overall IPL evidence. A strong powerplay result cannot establish death finishing. Check role duplication, same-role reserves, international workload, and an overseas slot's opportunity cost in a plausible XI. Use a squad scenario with ten names per role as candidate options. Keep any XI scenario within the applicable playing conditions.
7. Investigate the current disagreements explicitly. Contrast Eshan Malinga's thin overall positive rate and negative death rate with same-role alternatives. Compare Pathirana and Hazlewood with RR pace needs and conditional availability. Compare Varun and Kuldeep with the relevant middle-overs spin incumbents, recording Kuldeep's LSG trade and separate auction condition. Review Siraj's direct evidence and the India/overseas balance. For batting, check Bethell's sampled phases against the suggested No. 4–5 role, Inglis and Bairstow against domestic keeping cover, and Washington against comparable finishing or all-round deployment. Use the names to locate evidence gaps. Apply any ranking adjustment under the same documented rule to all eligible players.
8. Create a concise evidence card per player. Include action (retain, bid if released/registered, or another documented route), intended RR job, observed ordinary and pressure results, predicted pressure level and uncertainty, recent IPL exposure, alternative, and trigger that changes the recommendation. Label an overseas signing, price band or fitness condition as verified or scenario-based with a date and source. Refresh the release list when official data become available before the competition cutoff. The reported final IPL auction list follows Round 1, so pending registration or pool admission needs a conditional recommendation.

## Loop

Build both scenario tables from the common selector. Check every top-ten row against identity, pressure evidence, uncertainty, role and dated route. If a case is unconvincing, compare the next same-role alternative and identify the rule or model behavior that made the current player outrank them. If changing a rule, return to Stage 03 for replay and Stage 05 for robustness. Update a factual correction at the market or role source and regenerate downstream outputs.
