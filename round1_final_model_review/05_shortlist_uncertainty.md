# Stage 05 — Measure uncertainty in the actual shortlist

## Compute what the entrant needs

1. Measure three quantities independently. Forecast calibration checks how often realized opportunities fall in their predicted ranges. Evidence uncertainty describes the plausible range of a player's adjusted impact or a policy comparison. Rank stability measures how often a player remains in the top ten when data or assumptions are perturbed. A bootstrap inclusion frequency describes the pipeline under the stated resampling setup. Actual 2027 performance and auction availability remain future observations.
2. Replace or supplement the current 50-draw slate interval with paired whole-match resampling using shared draws for compared policies. Use a repeatable seed. For cheap evaluation use about 1,000 draws and check the two tails and stability across seeds or batch sizes. Report exact draw counts, valid draws, interval type and estimand. In each comparison distinguish a fixed assessable subset from changing eligibility inside the resample. Prefer an explicit pairwise difference interval on common blocks. For rank stability, refit or update model statistics and rerun the exact final selector per resample when computationally feasible. Start with a lower draw count for expensive fits, check convergence, and state which layers were refitted and which were fixed.
3. Preserve match clusters, competition and season structure. Resample the whole match for runs, wickets, and exposure together. Save per-pick top-ten inclusion frequency, median and central rank range among ranked draws, count of eligible draws, effective IPL exposure and the probability-style quantity's precise denominator. A player with very few eligible draws requires a prominent caveat. Report borderline reversals or tiers if score uncertainty makes exact ranks fragile. Retain a deterministic, declared tie-break for the final required order.
4. Compare the hard positive-IPL gate with one smoother uncertainty-aware alternative on the same forecasts and candidate pools. For example, shrink direct IPL phase impact toward the pre-cutoff IPL role mean using an empirically chosen pseudo-exposure, then use the shrunk estimate and its match-level uncertainty to flag a meaningful concern. The alternatives are evidence support and a justified ranking or eligibility rule, with thresholds declared before evaluating it. Show what happens near zero with low and high exposure and with genuinely negative observed IPL impact. Keep the prior policy as an explicit control. Choose from measured historical performance, exposure and stability with a record of retrospective policy development.
5. Run the **actual production shortlist** on P75/P80/P85, IPL-only, IPL plus T20I, recent-history, prior phase mix, chase-target proxy and strongest-match scenarios. Show raw model stability as its own diagnostic. For drop-one-match, identify both the standout match contribution and the observed rank sensitivity. Repeat the shortlist stability checks in each scenario that may be presented to RR. Save the decisive name changes and evidence. Do not interpret the current raw rank overlap of 5/10 and 2/10 as stability of the screened final lists.
6. Give each current or proposed pick a simple confidence label supported by observed exposure, agreement across replay, rank stability, and clearly stated caveats. Use numerical predictive uncertainty only where the calibration and sample size support it. Prioritize confidence in a role-appropriate group of picks over confidence in a close one-place rank difference. Link the confidence statement to the exact calculation and run.

## Loop

Start with the cheapest paired replay and fixed-policy sensitivities. If a decision is sensitive, identify the assumption or match that drives it. Refit only the affected layer to test the cause. If the evidence remains weak after the declared checks, record the pick as conditional or borderline and carry its role-matched reserve to Stage 06. Revisit Stage 03 if two policy comparisons use different rows without a declared reason.

## Exit and redirect

Save a per-player and per-policy uncertainty file, the exact production shortlist under the sensitivity views, and an evidence statement for every disputed boundary rank. Continue to [Stage 06](06_rr_decisions.md).
